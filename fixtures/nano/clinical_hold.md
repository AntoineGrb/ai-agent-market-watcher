NANOBIOTIX Announces FDA Clinical Hold on JNJ-1900 (NBTXR3) Clinical Program

PARIS and CAMBRIDGE, Mass. (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NANOBIOTIX (Euronext: NANO - NASDAQ: NBTX) today announced that the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has placed a clinical hold on the Investigational New Drug application for JNJ-1900 (NBTXR3), including the Phase 3 NANORAY-312 study, following reports of serious adverse events in patients treated in the study.

No new patients will be enrolled and dosing is paused while Johnson & Johnson, as sponsor, works with the FDA to address the agency's requests. The Company will provide an update when more information is available.
