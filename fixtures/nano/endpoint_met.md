NANOBIOTIX Announces That the Phase 3 NANORAY-312 Study of JNJ-1900 (NBTXR3) Met Its Primary Endpoint in Locally Advanced Head and Neck Cancer

PARIS and CAMBRIDGE, Mass. (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NANOBIOTIX (Euronext: NANO - NASDAQ: NBTX) today announced topline results from the Johnson & Johnson-led global Phase 3 NANORAY-312 study (NCT04892173) evaluating JNJ-1900 (NBTXR3) activated by radiotherapy, with or without cetuximab, in patients with platinum-ineligible locally advanced head and neck squamous cell carcinoma.

The study met its primary endpoint, demonstrating a statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in progression-free survival (PFS), as assessed by independent central review, compared with radiotherapy with or without cetuximab.

Overall survival, the key secondary endpoint, showed a favorable trend at the time of the final PFS analysis, and follow-up is ongoing. The safety profile of JNJ-1900 (NBTXR3) was consistent with previously reported data, with no new safety signals identified.

Detailed results will be submitted for presentation at an upcoming medical meeting. Johnson & Johnson intends to discuss the results with health authorities.

"This is a landmark moment for patients and for Nanobiotix," said Laurent Levy, Chief Executive Officer.
