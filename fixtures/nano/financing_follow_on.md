NANOBIOTIX Announces Pricing of Global Offering of Ordinary Shares and American Depositary Shares

PARIS and CAMBRIDGE, Mass. (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NANOBIOTIX (Euronext: NANO - NASDAQ: NBTX) today announced the pricing of its global offering of 6,800,000 new ordinary shares, including ordinary shares in the form of American Depositary Shares, at a price of €11.00 per share, for gross proceeds of approximately €74.8 million.

The Company has granted the underwriters a 30-day option to purchase up to 1,020,000 additional new ordinary shares. If the option is exercised in full, the global offering would result in the issuance of a maximum total of 7,820,000 new ordinary shares.

The Company intends to use the net proceeds to fund its research and development activities and for general corporate purposes, extending its cash runway.
