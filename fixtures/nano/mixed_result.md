NANOBIOTIX Reports Topline Results From Phase 3 NANORAY-312 Study

PARIS and CAMBRIDGE, Mass. (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NANOBIOTIX (Euronext: NANO - NASDAQ: NBTX) today reported topline results from the Johnson & Johnson-led Phase 3 NANORAY-312 study of JNJ-1900 (NBTXR3) in platinum-ineligible locally advanced head and neck squamous cell carcinoma.

The study met its primary endpoint of progression-free survival by independent central review. However, the analysis of overall survival, the key secondary endpoint, showed a numerical imbalance not in favor of the JNJ-1900 (NBTXR3) arm at this time (hazard ratio above 1). The companies believe the overall survival data are immature and further analyses are ongoing.

A higher rate of grade 3 or higher injection-site adverse events was observed in the JNJ-1900 (NBTXR3) arm.

Johnson & Johnson will review the totality of the data before determining regulatory next steps.
