NANOBIOTIX Announces New Data From Phase 1 Study of JNJ-1900 (NBTXR3) in Locally Advanced Pancreatic Cancer

PARIS and CAMBRIDGE, Mass. (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NANOBIOTIX (Euronext: NANO - NASDAQ: NBTX) today announced new data from the dose-escalation part of a Phase 1 study, sponsored by The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center, evaluating JNJ-1900 (NBTXR3) activated by radiation therapy in patients with borderline resectable or locally advanced pancreatic cancer, presented today at a medical congress.

In 20 evaluable patients, the treatment was feasible and well tolerated, with no dose-limiting toxicities. The recommended Phase 2 dose was established. Early signs of anti-tumor activity were observed, with a disease control rate of 85%.
