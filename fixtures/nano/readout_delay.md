Nanobiotix says NANORAY-312 readout now expected in 2029

Nanobiotix told investors that Johnson & Johnson now expects the final progression-free survival analysis of the Phase 3 NANORAY-312 study of JNJ-1900 (NBTXR3) in head and neck cancer in the first half of 2029, compared with previous guidance of the second half of 2028, as the accrual of PFS events has been slower than anticipated. The company said the trial design and primary endpoint are unchanged.
